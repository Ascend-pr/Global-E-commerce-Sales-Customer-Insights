# Global-E-commerce-Sales-Customer-Insights
---
📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes global e-commerce sales and customer behavior across multiple regions and product categories for the years 2023–2024. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides a clear view of revenue, profit, order trends, and customer insights, helping businesses identify growth opportunities and optimize decision-making.
---
🚀 Key Features

1. KPI Cards: Track total revenue, total orders, profit, and profit margin.

2. Sales Trend Analysis: Monthly revenue performance showing seasonal patterns and dips.

3. Regional Performance: Breakdown of revenue and profit across South America, North America, Africa, Europe, and Asia.

4. Category Insights: Comparison of sales across Electronics, Beauty, Home & Kitchen, Clothing, and Sports.

5. Quantity Analysis: Highlights which product categories are most popular by units sold.

6. Revenue & Profit by Region & Category: Matrix view to identify which products drive revenue in each region.
---
🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI – Dashboard creation and visualization.

- Excel/CSV Data – Source data for e-commerce transactions.

- Data Modeling – Used to create relationships, measures, and KPIs (DAX).
---
📈 Insights from the Dashboard

1. South America generated the highest revenue ($90K), while Europe had the lowest ($66K).

2. Electronics drove the most revenue ($85K), while Beauty and Home & Kitchen sold the highest quantities.

3. Revenue dipped mid-year (June–August) but stabilized towards the end.

4. Each region had unique top-performing categories, e.g., Beauty in Africa, Home & Kitchen in South America.
---
🎯 Use Cases

- Track global sales performance.

- Identify profitable regions and categories.

- Guide marketing campaigns and inventory planning.

- Support data-driven business decisions.
---
📌 How to Use

- Download the .pbix file (Power BI report).

- Explore the interactive dashboard by filtering regions, categories, and timeframes.
